About Me

Hello! I'm Sharon Pricilla, a Computer Science Engineering (CSE) student with a strong passion for creativity, technology, and digital design. Alongside my academic journey, I have built Skyline Visuals, where I provide freelance design services for businesses, creators, students, and entrepreneurs. My passion for designing started with creating creative visual content and has grown into a professional freelancing service. I enjoy turning ideas into eye-catching designs that help people communicate their message effectively. I specialize in logo design, posters, invitation cards, social media graphics, flyers, menu cards, branding materials, and video editing. As a Computer Science Engineering student, I combine technical thinking with creativity to produce designs that are both visually appealing and professionally structured. My goal is to continuously learn, improve, and help businesses build a strong visual identity. Through Skyline Visuals, I strive to provide affordable, high-quality, and unique designs while building long-term relationships with clients.
